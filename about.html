How We Started

CarePlus Pharmacy was started to give the neighbourhood a pharmacy people can rely on — genuine products, fair prices, and a pharmacist who takes the time to explain how to use what you're buying.

We keep a steady stock of everyday medicines, supplements and healthcare essentials, and if we don't have something in stock, we'll help you find it.

Why Customers Choose Us

Every product on our shelves is sourced from licensed, trusted suppliers. Our pharmacist is available to answer questions about dosage, interactions, and which product suits your symptoms.

We also offer delivery within Dar es Salaam and quick ordering over WhatsApp, so getting what you need doesn't mean a long wait in line.
